Design systemVigil design systemA cleaner, Apple-like console: calm surfaces, one accent, and status shown the same way everywhere. Dark is the default for the SOC; light is included and switchable from the profile menu.
Plus Jakarta SansRoboto MonoDark and light
01Principles
Calm by defaultOne accent colour. Status colour appears only when something needs attention. Soft surfaces, 12–14 px corners, no glow or scanlines.
Every number has a limitShow a value against its limit (214 of 300) with Good, Fair or Poor, so nobody has to know what is normal.
Say where it comes fromEvery widget has an ⓘ button: the data it reads, the math, and the limit it is judged against.
Plain wordsName what a thing is, not how it works. No internal names on screen: Envelope, claim rate, admission control.

Layout
Frame 1440 × 900. Top bar in two rows: actions (52 px) and page tabs (46 px), grouped Monitor, Investigate, Configure.Ask Vigil docks on the right at 400 px and follows you to every page, including setup. History is kept per page and per case.A case opens over the page at 76% of the width (about three quarters), or full screen.The Needs you island sits in the top bar on every page: hover for a preview, click for the full list, with this page’s items first.Confirmations drop from the island with Undo and a fuse line for the undo window; in full-screen Overview they sit bottom centre.
Voice
Say what it is and what to do next: “Okta needs a fix”, then a button that opens Okta.Numbers come with their limit and a level: “214 of 300 · Good”.Use the same word for the same thing on every page (see the vocabulary on the handoff board).Prefer verbs people use: “Ask first”, “On their own”, “Picked up automatically”.Explain consequences before irreversible actions, and never hide who or what made a decision.
08Built on shadcn/uiRecommended as the foundation. Vigil’s web client today is React 18, Vite and Tailwind 3.4, with React Flow (@xyflow/react) for the workflow builder and react-markdown, and no Radix or shadcn yet.
shadcn/ui is copy-in components: Radix primitives styled with Tailwind, under the MIT licence, which is compatible with Vigil’s Apache-2.0. Keep its MIT notice.Use the new-york style with Vigil’s tokens, so the look stays calm and Apple-like.The interaction patterns in section 05 are Vigil’s own components, composed from shadcn primitives and Tailwind animations.
Why shadcn/ui: compared with MUI and daisyUI
OptionFit for VigilWhy
shadcn/uiui.shadcn.comGood fit · recommendedTailwind is already in Vigil. Radix gives accessible menus, popovers, dialogs and Command (⌘K). We own the code, so our tokens and the calm look apply everywhere. MIT, which fits Apache-2.0.
MUImui.comPoor fitThe most complete, with a data grid and date pickers. But Material styling and Emotion CSS-in-JS fight the Tailwind stack and the calm look, and the advanced data grid and date pickers are paid (MUI X Pro and Premium). If we need a heavy grid, TanStack Table under shadcn’s Table is lighter.
daisyUIdaisyui.comFair fitQuick Tailwind theming with class names like btn and card. But it is mostly CSS, without accessible menus, popovers, comboboxes or a command palette, so we would still need Radix. Its default look is more playful.
